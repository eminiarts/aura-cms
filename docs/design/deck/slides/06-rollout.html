{{top:Open questions}}

Decided, and still to decide

What the v1 review and the page validation settled, and the design calls that need Enes and Bajram.

Decided in v2
One control height, one field radius

--aura-control-height (28 · 32 · 36) and --aura-control-radius for buttons, inputs, selects and pickers.

Tabs

Underline tabs split a form; segmented tabs switch filters and views.

Tables

The card-less list with hairlines, avatars and typed headings is the resource table.

One error style

Red ring on the field, one message style (red-600 / red-400, AA).

Needs a decision
One tint recipe

Status pills, trend pills, chips, alerts and avatars use five fill/text pairs (100/700, 50/700, 100/800, 50/800…). Pick one for light and one for dark.

Neutrals

The default gray is Tailwind gray up to 600 and slate from 700, so dark surfaces shift hue. Keep it, or use one family?

Disabled

Fields are a fill (value stays readable), buttons fade to 50 %. Keep both, or one rule?

Button names

button = button.primary; danger is the tint, error the solid. Proposal: primary, danger, danger-light.

Light-mode contrast

Field ring (1.25:1) and the off toggle (1.24:1) are below 3:1; raising them changes the light look.
